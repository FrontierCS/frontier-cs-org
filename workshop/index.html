ProposalICLR 2027 workshop

How Far Are AI Agents from Becoming Computer Scientists?

Agents make progress when a person specifies the task and supplies a verifier on every answer. Real research often gives neither. This workshop is about what an agent does then.

We have proposed this workshop and its challenge for ICLR 2027. ICLR decides on November 29, 2026.

1

What to improve

  • Task given
  • Feedback verifiable

Today’s successes. How do harnesses, teams of agents, people in the loop and post-training get more out of the same setup?

2

What to trust

  • Task given
  • Feedback sparse

Most real research. How does an agent verify, replicate and try to refute a result it produced itself?

3

What to explore

  • No task
  • No feedback

The last mile. How does an agent choose a question, judge its novelty and decide when to drop it?
