Methodology

Score and pass are separate. Scores describe solution quality; pass/fail checks the task's scientific acceptance criteria. Scores are task-specific and can exceed 100.

Submission measurements. Curves connect observed submissions within the selected train or final split. They do not imply scores between measurements. Final scores are never replaced with the best training score.

Comparison coverage. Model summaries show scored-task coverage. The comparable mean uses only tasks with final scores for every displayed model. Missing token or time measurements stay unavailable.

Tokens and submissions. Tokens sum provider-reported input and output across calls, including cached prompt tokens where reported. They are not unique context length. Submissions count distinct training verification requests (including failed requests), plus the final evaluation when scored.

Publication status. Refresh checks for a newer publication, not a live connection to evaluation infrastructure. Operational metadata stays in the internal evaluation CSV. This unlisted page is public, not access-controlled.
